01 / The person behind the work

About

A creative practice shaped by cinema, music and the details of sound.

A filmmaker's eye.
A musician's ear.
A storyteller's instinct.

I'm Sarovar Ramachandra, a filmmaker and audio professional interested in the relationship between image, music and emotion.

My background includes a Professional Diploma in Sound Recording & Engineering and Dolby Atmos Essential certification, alongside hands-on experience in studio recording and post-production.

I continue to explore storytelling through short films, original music and sound — with a focus on building work that feels honest, immersive and memorable.
